All exports are synchronous.

## GetGarages

Copies of the configured garages (invalid rows are dropped at start).

```lua
exports.opx_garage:GetGarages()
```

**Returns**

- garages: `table[]`
  - name: `string`
  - label: `string`
  - type: `'public'` or `'impound'`
  - x, y, z: `number`
  - radius: `number`
  - bucket: `integer`
  - spawns: `{ x, y, z, heading }[]`

## OpenGarage

Opens a garage menu for a player without the position check (phone app, job). Its actions are accepted anywhere for 5 minutes; cars still come out on that garage's bays and are stored only near it.

```lua
exports.opx_garage:OpenGarage(source, name)
```

- source: `integer`
- name: `string`
  - A `Config.garages` name.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `'invalid_player'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'unknown_garage'`

## Impound

Sends an owned car that is out to the impound garage. Occupants are not checked. The owner is told when online.

```lua
exports.opx_garage:Impound(plateOrVehicleId, bySource)
```

- plateOrVehicleId: `string` or `integer`
  - A plate, or a live vehicle id.
- bySource?: `integer`
  - Passed on in `opx:garage:impounded`.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'no_impound'`, `'invalid_plate'`, `'invalid_vehicle'`, `'not_owned'`, `'not_out'`, or an opx_core `StoreOwnedVehicle` refusal

## GetVehicleGarage

Garage name of a stored car. `'default'` or a name no longer configured counts as any public garage.

```lua
exports.opx_garage:GetVehicleGarage(plate)
```

- plate: `string`

**Returns**

- garage?: `string`
  - `nil` when the car is out or unknown.
