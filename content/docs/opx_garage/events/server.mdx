### opx:garage:taken

A player took a car out of a garage.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:garage:taken', function(source, plate, garage, vehicleId) end)
```

- source: `integer`
- plate: `string`
- garage: `string`
- vehicleId: `integer`

### opx:garage:stored

A player stored a car.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:garage:stored', function(source, plate, garage) end)
```

- source: `integer`
- plate: `string`
- garage: `string`

### opx:garage:impounded

A car went to the impound (staff command, `Impound` export, or its owner from the garage menu).

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:garage:impounded', function(plate, bySource) end)
```

- plate: `string`
- bySource?: `integer`
