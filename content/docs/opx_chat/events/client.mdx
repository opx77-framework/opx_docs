### open77:chat:visibility

The composer opened or closed.

```lua
AddEventHandler('open77:chat:visibility', function(open) end)
```

- open: `boolean`

### chat:commandSubmitted

A slash line that is not a channel, before it runs.

```lua
AddEventHandler('chat:commandSubmitted', function(raw, tokens) end)
```

- raw: `string`
- tokens: `string[]`

## Handled events

```lua
TriggerEvent('chat:open')        -- also 'open77:chat:open'
TriggerEvent('chat:close')
TriggerEvent('chat:addMessage', message)
TriggerEvent('open77:chat:requestVisibility') -- answers with open77:chat:visibility
```
