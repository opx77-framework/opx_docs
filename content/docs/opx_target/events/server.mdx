### option.serverEvent

Sent to the server when an option with `serverEvent = '<name>'` is selected. The name is yours; validate everything in the handler.

```lua
RegisterNetEvent('myres:target:rob', function(targetId, info)
    local src = source
end)
```

- targetId?: `number` or `string`
  - The target's `playerId`, `vehicleId` or `npcId`, or its `propId` (decimal string). `nil` for a surface or the sky.
- info: `table`
  - kind: `string`
  - option: `string`
    - The option's `name`.
  - model?: `string`
  - position?: `{ x, y, z }`
    - Hit point. `nil` for the sky.
  - distance?: `number`
  - direction?: `{ x, y, z }`
    - Sky only: the ray's unit direction.
  - data?: `table`
    - Copy of the option's `data`.
