---
title: Client
---

With the client library, prefer `OPX.OnPlayerLoaded`, `OPX.OnPlayerData`, `OPX.OnJobChanged` and `OPX.OnMoneyChanged`.

### opx:playerLoaded

```lua
RegisterNetEvent('opx:playerLoaded', function(data) end)
```

- data: `table` (PlayerData)

### opx:playerUnloaded

```lua
RegisterNetEvent('opx:playerUnloaded', function() end)
```

### opx:setPlayerData

One PlayerData field changed.

```lua
RegisterNetEvent('opx:setPlayerData', function(key, value) end)
```

- key: `'accounts'`, `'job'`, `'metadata'`, `'isNew'`, `'family'`
- value: `any` (the whole new value of that field)
