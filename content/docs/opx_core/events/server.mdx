---
title: Server
---

Host events: listen with `AddEventHandler`, never `RegisterNetEvent`.

### opx:playerLoaded

A character finished loading.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:playerLoaded', function(source, data) end)
```

- source: `number`
- data: `table` (PlayerData, see `GetPlayer`)

### opx:playerUnloaded

Relog, character switch or disconnect.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:playerUnloaded', function(source, citizenId) end)
```

- source: `number`
- citizenId: `string`

### opx:characterCreated

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:characterCreated', function(source, citizenId) end)
```

- source: `number`
- citizenId: `string`

### opx:characterDeleted

Delete your own rows about that character here.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:characterDeleted', function(source, citizenId) end)
```

- source: `number`
- citizenId: `string`

### opx:moneyChanged

Every player balance change, raised before the export returns.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:moneyChanged', function(source, account, balance, delta, reason) end)
```

- source: `number`
- account: `string`
- balance: `number`
- delta: `number`
- reason: `string`

### opx:societyMoneyChanged

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:societyMoneyChanged', function(job, balance, delta, reason) end)
```

- job: `string`
- balance: `number`
- delta: `number`
- reason: `string`

### opx:jobChanged

New job or grade, duty change, or the job registered / unregistered by its resource.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:jobChanged', function(source, job, previous) end)
```

- source: `number`
- job: `table`
- previous: `table`

### opx:metadataChanged

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:metadataChanged', function(source, key, value) end)
```

- source: `number`
- key: `string`
- value: `any` (`nil` when removed)

### opx:jobRegistered

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:jobRegistered', function(name, job) end)
```

- name: `string`
- job: `table` (definition plus `name` and `resource`)

### opx:jobUnregistered

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:jobUnregistered', function(name) end)
```

- name: `string`

### opx:vehicleSpawned

A car spawned through opx_core.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:vehicleSpawned', function(vehicleId, plate, citizenId) end)
```

- vehicleId: `number`
- plate?: `string`
- citizenId?: `string`

### opx:vehicleStored

An owned car went back to its garage.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:vehicleStored', function(plate) end)
```

- plate: `string`

### opx:ownedVehicleAdded

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:ownedVehicleAdded', function(plate, citizenId, record) end)
```

- plate: `string`
- citizenId: `string`
- record: `string`

### opx:ownedVehicleRemoved

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:ownedVehicleRemoved', function(plate, citizenId) end)
```

- plate: `string`
- citizenId: `string`
