### opx:rental:rented

A car was rented.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:rental:rented', function(source, vehicleId, record, price) end)
```

- source: `integer`
- vehicleId: `integer`
- record: `string`
- price: `integer`
  - Without the deposit.

### opx:rental:returned

A car was returned at a desk, or `EndRental` was called.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:rental:returned', function(source, vehicleId, refunded) end)
```

- source: `integer`
- vehicleId: `integer`
- refunded: `boolean`

### opx:rental:expired

The rental ended without a return (time over, renter gone too long, or the car disappeared). The deposit is lost.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:rental:expired', function(citizenId, vehicleId) end)
```

- citizenId: `string`
- vehicleId: `integer`
