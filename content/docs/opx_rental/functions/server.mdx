## GetRental

The running rental of a player.

```lua
exports.opx_rental:GetRental(source)
```

- source: `integer`

**Returns**

- rental?: `table`
  - vehicleId: `integer`
  - plate: `string`
  - record: `string`
  - secondsLeft: `integer`
    - `0` when time is over and the car is waiting to be empty.
  - deposit: `integer`

## EndRental

Ends a player's rental now. The car is removed wherever it is, even occupied. Notifies the player and triggers `opx:rental:returned`.

```lua
exports.opx_rental:EndRental(source, refund)
```

- source: `integer`
- refund?: `boolean`
  - `true` gives the deposit back to the account that paid.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `'invalid_source'`, `'no_rental'`
