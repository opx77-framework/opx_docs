title: Server
---

Raised after the job change was made through opx_core. Deposits and withdrawals raise opx_core's `opx:societyMoneyChanged` with reason `'society:deposit'` or `'society:withdraw'`.

### opx:society:hired

The target accepted the offer and is now grade 0.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:society:hired', function(bossSource, targetSource, job) end)
```

- bossSource: `number`
- targetSource: `number`
- job: `string`

### opx:society:fired

The target now has `Config.fallbackJob`, grade 0.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:society:fired', function(bossSource, targetSource, job) end)
```

- bossSource: `number`
- targetSource: `number`
- job: `string`

### opx:society:gradeChanged

After a promote or demote.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:society:gradeChanged', function(bossSource, targetSource, job, grade) end)
```

- bossSource: `number`
- targetSource: `number`
- job: `string`
- grade: `integer`
  - The new grade.
