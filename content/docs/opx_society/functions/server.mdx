Every export is synchronous. Job resources never name the provider:

```lua
local society = exports.opx_core:GetProvider('society')
local ok, isBoss = pcall(Open77.exports.callSync, society, 'IsBoss', source, 'police')
```

## OpenBossMenu

Opens the boss menu for a player who holds the job at boss grade. No location check: you decide where. The menu then stays bound to where it opened (`Config.sessionDistance`).

```lua
exports.opx_society:OpenBossMenu(source, jobName)
```

- source: `number`
- jobName?: `string`
  - Default: the player's own job.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'not_loaded'`, `'unknown_job'`, `'wrong_job'`, `'not_boss'`, `'position_unknown'`

## IsBoss

Whether a loaded character holds the job at its boss grade or above. Duty does not matter.

```lua
exports.opx_society:IsBoss(source, jobName)
```

- source: `number`
- jobName?: `string`
  - Default: the player's own job.

**Returns**

- isBoss: `boolean`

## GetBossGrade

Boss grade of a registered job: `data.bossGrade` from opx_core's `RegisterJob` when it is one of its grades, else its highest grade.

```lua
exports.opx_society:GetBossGrade(jobName)
```

- jobName: `string`

**Returns**

- grade?: `integer`
  - `nil` for an unknown job.
