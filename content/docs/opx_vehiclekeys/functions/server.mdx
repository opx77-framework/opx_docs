All exports are synchronous. Other resources call them through the provider, so a replacement works:

```lua
local keys = exports.opx_core:GetProvider('vehiclekeys')
local ok, has = pcall(Open77.exports.callSync, keys, 'HasKey', source, vehicleId)
```

A vehicle id is a live Open77 vehicle id (`integer`), a plate an opx_core plate (`string`).

## HasKey

Whether the player's character owns the car, holds a stored or temporary key to it, or has `Config.staffRight`.

```lua
exports.opx_vehiclekeys:HasKey(source, vehicleId)
```

- source: `integer`
- vehicleId: `integer`

**Returns**

- hasKey: `boolean`

## GiveKey

Gives a key to the character of `source`. Already holding it or owning the car returns `true`.

```lua
exports.opx_vehiclekeys:GiveKey(source, vehicleIdOrPlate, options)
```

- source: `integer`
- vehicleIdOrPlate: `integer` or `string`
- options?: `table`
  - temporary?: `boolean`
    - `true`: memory-only key on the live car (rental, test drive), dropped with the car.
    - Otherwise a stored key on the plate, owned cars only.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_player'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'unknown_vehicle'`, `'not_owned'`, `'too_many'`, `'not_ready'`

## RemoveKey

Removes the stored and temporary keys a character holds to a car. Works on offline characters.

```lua
exports.opx_vehiclekeys:RemoveKey(sourceOrCitizenId, vehicleIdOrPlate)
```

- sourceOrCitizenId: `integer` or `string`
- vehicleIdOrPlate: `integer` or `string`

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_player'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'unknown_vehicle'`, `'no_key'`

## GetKeyHolders

Citizen ids holding a stored key to the plate, sorted. The owner and temporary keys are not listed.

```lua
exports.opx_vehiclekeys:GetKeyHolders(plate)
```

- plate: `string`

**Returns**

- citizenIds: `string[]`

## SetLocked

Sets the lock of a car. Key holders keep their access.

```lua
exports.opx_vehiclekeys:SetLocked(vehicleId, locked)
```

- vehicleId: `integer`
- locked: `boolean`

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_vehicle'`, `'invalid_argument'`, or an `Open77.vehicles.setLocked` refusal

## IsLocked

The lock state of a car.

```lua
exports.opx_vehiclekeys:IsLocked(vehicleId)
```

- vehicleId: `integer`

**Returns**

- locked?: `boolean`
  - `nil` for an unknown vehicle.
