---
title: Server
---

`bySource` is the player who acted, `nil` when an export did it.

### opx:vehiclekeys:lockChanged

A car was locked or unlocked.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:vehiclekeys:lockChanged', function(vehicleId, locked, bySource) end)
```

- vehicleId: `integer`
- locked: `boolean`
- bySource?: `integer`

### opx:vehiclekeys:keyGiven

A character received a key.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:vehiclekeys:keyGiven', function(plateOrVehicleId, citizenId, bySource, temporary) end)
```

- plateOrVehicleId: `string` or `integer`
  - The plate for a stored key, the vehicle id for a temporary key.
- citizenId: `string`
- bySource?: `integer`
- temporary: `boolean`

### opx:vehiclekeys:keyRemoved

A key was taken back. Not triggered when keys go with a removed car or a deleted character.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:vehiclekeys:keyRemoved', function(plateOrVehicleId, citizenId, bySource) end)
```

- plateOrVehicleId: `string` or `integer`
- citizenId: `string`
- bySource?: `integer`
