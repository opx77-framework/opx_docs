Both are async: call them from a thread.

```lua
local skin = Open77.exports.callSync('opx_core', 'GetProvider', 'skin')
```

## GetGarments

Garments of a slot sorted by label. The first call per slot and family builds the list.

```lua
Open77.exports.call(skin, 'GetGarments', slot, offset, limit, family):await()
```

- slot: `string`
  - `'Head'`, `'Face'`, `'InnerChest'`, `'OuterChest'`, `'Legs'`, `'Feet'`, `'Outfit'`
- offset?: `number` default `0`
- limit?: `number` 1 to 60, default `60`
- family?: `'male'` or `'female'` default: the local body's

**Returns**

- garments?: `table`
  - total: `number` garments in the whole list
  - items: `{ record: string, label: string }[]`
- reason?: `'invalid_slot'`, `'invalid_range'` or another reason

## FindGarment

1-based position of a record in the `GetGarments` order, or `nil`.

```lua
Open77.exports.call(skin, 'FindGarment', slot, record, family):await()
```

- slot: `string`
- record: `string`
- family?: `'male'` or `'female'`

**Returns**

- position?: `number`
