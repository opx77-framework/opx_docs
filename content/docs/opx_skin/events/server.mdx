### opx:skin:clothingChanged

Triggered after a successful `SetClothing`.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:skin:clothingChanged', function(playerId, slots) end)
```

- playerId: `number`
- slots: `table` the 7 saved slots

### opx:skin:uniformChanged

Triggered when a uniform is put on or removed.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:skin:uniformChanged', function(playerId, owner) end)
```

- playerId: `number`
- owner?: `string` resource of the uniform, `nil` when it is gone
