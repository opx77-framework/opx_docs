### opx:inventory:changed

Triggered once per change of a loaded character's pockets (exports, menu, stash, commands, starter kit). Not for stash-only changes, ammo burnt by a weapon, or `clearinventory`.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:inventory:changed', function(source, item, delta) end)
```

- source: `number`
- item: `string`
- delta: `integer`
  - Positive when added, negative when taken.

### opx:inventory:itemRegistered

Triggered after each successful `RegisterItem` (new or replaced).

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:inventory:itemRegistered', function(item, resource) end)
```

- item: `string`
- resource: `string`
  - The owner.

### opx:inventory:itemUnregistered

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:inventory:itemUnregistered', function(item) end)
```

- item: `string`
