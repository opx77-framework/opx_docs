## GetBalance

Cash and bank of a loaded character.

```lua
exports.opx_bank:GetBalance(source)
```

- source: `number`

**Returns**

- balance?: `{ cash: number, bank: number }`
- reason?: `'not_loaded'`

## Transfer

Bank to bank between two loaded characters, all or nothing. No location check: the caller decides where it may happen.

```lua
exports.opx_bank:Transfer(fromSource, toSource, amount, note)
```

- fromSource: `number`
- toSource: `number`
- amount: `number`
  - Integer between `Config.transferMin` and `Config.transferMax`.
- note?: `string`
  - Up to `Config.noteMax` bytes.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_amount'`, `'insufficient_funds'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'target_not_loaded'`, `'self_transfer'`, `'busy'`, `'core_unavailable'`

```lua
local ok, reason = exports.opx_bank:Transfer(source, target, 500, 'rent')
if not ok then print(reason) end
```
