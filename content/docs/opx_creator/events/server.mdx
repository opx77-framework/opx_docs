---
title: Server
---

Exactly one of `saved` or `closed` ends every session. The last argument `info` is shared by the three events:

- token: `string`
- mode: `'creation'` or `'edit'`
- tag?: `string`
- opener: `string`
- scope?: `string`
- changed?: `table` (on `saved` only)
  - body: `boolean`
  - clothing: `string[]` equipment slots saved, e.g. `{ 'Legs' }`

### opx:creator:opened

The creator is on screen (again after a re-open).

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:creator:opened', function(playerId, info) end)
```

- playerId: `number`
- info: `table`

### opx:creator:saved

The look was kept. Charge here when `info.opener` is you and `info.changed.clothing` is not empty.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:creator:saved', function(playerId, characterKey, family, info) end)
```

- playerId: `number`
- characterKey: `string` citizen id
- family: `'male'` or `'female'`
- info: `table`

### opx:creator:closed

Closed without a save.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:creator:closed', function(playerId, reason, info) end)
```

- playerId: `number`
- reason: `string`
  - `'cancelled'`, `'failed'`, `'no_answer'`, `'dropped'`, `'stopped'`, `'closed'`, `'character_changed'`, `'unchanged'`, or the `Close` reason
- info: `table`

<Callout type="warn">
Body edits (barber, surgery): a player who disconnects mid-edit keeps the last preview without `saved`. Charge before opening and refund on `closed`.
</Callout>
