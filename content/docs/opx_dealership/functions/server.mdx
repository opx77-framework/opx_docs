## GetCatalogue

Copies of the catalogue rows. Rows whose record the platform does not know are dropped at start.

```lua
exports.opx_dealership:GetCatalogue()
```

**Returns**

- catalogue: `table[]`
  - index: `integer`
  - record: `string`
    - `Vehicle.*` TweakDB record.
  - label: `string`
  - category: `string`
  - price: `integer`

## IsOnDuty

Whether the player holds the `cardealer` job and is on duty.

```lua
exports.opx_dealership:IsOnDuty(source)
```

- source: `integer`

**Returns**

- onDuty: `boolean`
