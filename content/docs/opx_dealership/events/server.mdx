### opx:dealership:sold

After every sale (dealer or self-service), once the money moved and the owned car exists.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:dealership:sold', function(sellerSource, customerSource, record, plate, price) end)
```

- sellerSource: `integer` or `false`
  - `false` for a self-service purchase.
- customerSource: `integer`
- record: `string`
- plate: `string`
- price: `integer`

### opx:dealership:duty

A `cardealer` went on or off duty (desk, staff `setjob`, job change, `Config.dutyOffOnLoad`).

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:dealership:duty', function(source, onDuty) end)
```

- source: `integer`
- onDuty: `boolean`
