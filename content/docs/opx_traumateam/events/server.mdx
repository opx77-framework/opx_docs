### opx:traumateam:subscribed

A plan was bought or extended.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:traumateam:subscribed', function(source, tier, expiresAt) end)
```

- source: `number`
- tier: `string`
- expiresAt: `integer`

### opx:traumateam:revived

A Trauma Team revive.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:traumateam:revived', function(medic, patient, fee, covered) end)
```

- medic: `number`
- patient: `number`
- fee: `integer`
  - Before coverage.
- covered: `integer`

### opx:traumateam:treated

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:traumateam:treated', function(medic, patient, fee) end)
```

- medic: `number`
- patient: `number`
- fee: `integer`

### opx:traumateam:duty

A member's service started or ended (station, staff command, fired).

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:traumateam:duty', function(source, onDuty) end)
```

- source: `number`
- onDuty: `boolean`
