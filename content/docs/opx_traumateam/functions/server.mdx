Every export is synchronous: `exports.opx_traumateam:Name(...)` or `Open77.exports.callSync('opx_traumateam', 'Name', ...)`.

## GetSubscription

The active plan of a player or of any character.

```lua
exports.opx_traumateam:GetSubscription(sourceOrCitizenId)
```

- sourceOrCitizenId: `number` or `string`

**Returns**

- subscription?: `table`
  - tier: `string`
    - A key of `Config.tiers` (`'silver'`, `'gold'`, `'platinum'`).
  - label: `string`
  - expiresAt: `integer`
    - Unix seconds.
  - priority: `integer`
    - Lower = higher in the call list.

## GetTiers

The plans in menu order (copies of `Config.tiers`).

```lua
exports.opx_traumateam:GetTiers()
```

**Returns**

- tiers: `table[]`
  - name: `string`
  - label: `string`
  - price: `integer`
  - days: `integer`
  - priority: `integer`
  - reviveCoverage: `number`
    - 0 to 1, share of the revive fee paid by the plan.
  - respawnCoverage: `number`
    - 0 to 1, share of the hospital bill.
  - reviveHealth: `number`
    - 0 to 1, health after a Trauma Team revive.

## IsOnDuty

Whether a player is a Trauma Team member on duty.

```lua
exports.opx_traumateam:IsOnDuty(source)
```

- source: `number`

**Returns**

- onDuty: `boolean`

## GetDispatch

The open calls, best plan first, then oldest.

```lua
exports.opx_traumateam:GetDispatch()
```

**Returns**

- calls: `table[]`
  - id: `integer`
    - The medical provider's call id.
  - patient: `number`
  - priority: `integer`
    - Tier priority, or `Config.uninsuredPriority`.
  - tier?: `string`
  - position: `{ x: number, y: number, z: number }`
  - bucket: `integer`
  - at: `integer`
  - responder?: `number`
