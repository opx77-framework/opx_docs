### opx:character:phaseChanged

Triggered on every phase change. Order: `joining`, `selecting`, `leaving`, `creating` (new character only), `playing`. A logout goes `playing`, `leaving`, `joining`, `selecting`.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:character:phaseChanged', function(source, phase, previous) end)
```

- source: `number`
- phase: `string`
- previous: `string`

### opx:character:spawned

Triggered when a character enters the world.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:character:spawned', function(source, citizenId, info) end)
```

- source: `number`
- citizenId: `string`
- info: `table`
  - citizenId: `string`
  - creation: `boolean` first spawn after the creator
  - resumed: `boolean` opx_character restarted while the player was in the world (no placement)

### opx:character:loggedOut

Triggered after a logout went through.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:character:loggedOut', function(source, citizenId) end)
```

- source: `number`
- citizenId: `string`
