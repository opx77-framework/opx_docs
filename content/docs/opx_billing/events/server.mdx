### opx:billing:created

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:billing:created', function(billId, citizenId, job, amount, issuerSource) end)
```

- billId: `string`
- citizenId: `string`
  - The debtor.
- job: `string`
- amount: `integer`
- issuerSource?: `number`

### opx:billing:paid

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:billing:paid', function(billId, source, job, amount) end)
```

- billId: `string`
- source: `number`
  - The payer.
- job: `string`
- amount: `integer`

### opx:billing:cancelled

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:billing:cancelled', function(billId, bySource) end)
```

- billId: `string`
- bySource?: `number`
  - `nil` when cancelled through `CancelBill`.
