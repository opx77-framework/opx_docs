Every export is synchronous: `exports.opx_billing:Name(...)` or `Open77.exports.callSync('opx_billing', 'Name', ...)`.

## CreateBill

Creates an unpaid bill from a job to a loaded player and tells him. No distance and no job rule: the caller decides who may bill (`Config.jobs` only applies to `/bill` and the eye).

```lua
exports.opx_billing:CreateBill(targetSource, job, amount, label, issuerSource)
```

- targetSource: `number`
  - The player who owes.
- job: `string`
  - A job registered in opx_core. Its society is credited.
- amount: `integer`
  - 1 to `Config.maxAmount`.
- label: `string`
  - 3 to 64 characters.
- issuerSource?: `number`
  - The employee. His name is kept; he gets `Config.commission` when online.

**Returns**

- billId: `string` or `false`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'not_ready'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'unknown_job'`, `'invalid_amount'`, `'invalid_label'`, `'too_many'`, `'busy'`

```lua
local id, reason = exports.opx_billing:CreateBill(target, 'mechanic', 450, 'Repair', source)
```

## GetBills

Unpaid bills of a player, newest first. Empty when unknown.

```lua
exports.opx_billing:GetBills(sourceOrCitizenId)
```

- sourceOrCitizenId: `number` or `string`

**Returns**

- bills: `table[]`
  - id: `string`
  - job: `string`
  - amount: `integer`
  - label: `string`
  - issuerName?: `string`
  - createdAt: `integer`
    - Unix seconds.

## PayBill

The player pays one of his bills from the first account of `Config.payFrom` that holds the whole amount; the society is credited in the same step. No location check.

```lua
exports.opx_billing:PayBill(source, billId)
```

- source: `number`
- billId: `string`

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'not_ready'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'not_found'`, `'not_yours'`, `'busy'`, `'insufficient_funds'`, `'society_unavailable'`, `'core_unavailable'`

## CancelBill

Removes an unpaid bill; nobody pays. The debtor is told when online.

```lua
exports.opx_billing:CancelBill(billId)
```

- billId: `string`

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `'not_found'`
