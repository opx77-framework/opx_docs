### opx:admin:audit

Every accepted staff action (not raised for refusals or failures).

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:admin:audit', function(staff, action, detail) end)
```

- staff: `number`
- action: `string` (`'tool player.ban'`, `'menu opened'`, `'noclip on'`, `'action <id>'`...)
- detail: `string` (up to 255 bytes, `''` when none)

### opx:admin:sanction

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:admin:sanction', function(staff, kind, userId, name, reason, seconds) end)
```

- staff: `number`
- kind: `'warn'`, `'kick'`, `'ban'`, `'unban'`
- userId: `string`
- name: `string` (account name)
- reason: `string` (`''` for an unban)
- seconds: `number` (ban duration; `0` = permanent or not a ban)

### opx:admin:report

A player sent `/report`.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:admin:report', function(source, ticketId, text) end)
```

- source: `number`
- ticketId: `number`
- text: `string`
