Every export is synchronous. Never name the resource, ask opx_core:

```lua
local medical = exports.opx_core:GetProvider('medical')
if medical then
  local ok, down = pcall(Open77.exports.callSync, medical, 'IsDown', source)
end
```

## IsDown

```lua
exports.opx_medical:IsDown(source)
```

- source: `number`

**Returns**

- down: `boolean`

## GetState

The downed state, or `nil` when the player is not down.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:GetState(source)
```

- source: `number`

**Returns**

- state?: `table`
  - down: `true`
  - at: `integer`
    - Unix seconds when he went down.
  - cause: `string`
    - `'unknown'`, `'firearm'`, `'melee'`, `'explosion'`, `'fall'`, `'vehicle'`, `'environment'`, `'script'`
  - position?: `{ x: number, y: number, z: number }`
  - respawnIn: `integer`
    - Seconds before the hospital respawn is allowed, `0` = now.
  - called: `boolean`
  - responder?: `number`

## Revive

Revives any dead player where he fell. `opx:medical:up` with reason `'revived'` follows.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:Revive(source, options)
```

- source: `number`
- options?: `table`
  - health?: `number`
    - Fraction 0.05 to 1, default `Config.revive.health`.
  - graceMs?: `integer`
    - Invulnerable window, 0 to 60000, default `Config.revive.graceMs`.
  - by?: `number`
    - The reviver, passed on with `opx:medical:up`.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_player'`, `'not_down'`, `'refused:<platform reason>'`

## Respawn

Respawns a downed player at a hospital: bill (bank first, then cash, coverage applied, never blocks), society credit, respawn. A refused respawn gives the money back.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:Respawn(source, options)
```

- source: `number`
- options?: `table`
  - force?: `boolean`
    - Skip the countdown and `Config.respawn.enabled`.
  - free?: `boolean`
    - No hospital bill.
  - hospital?: `integer`
    - Index in `Config.hospitals`, default the nearest to where he fell.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_player'`, `'not_down'`, `'busy'`, `'respawn_disabled'`, `'too_early'`, `'no_hospital'`, `'refused:<platform reason>'`

## SetCoverage

Sets or clears the hospital-bill coverage of a loaded player. One per player, owned by the calling resource; cleared on unload, disconnect and when the owner stops.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:SetCoverage(source, coverage)
```

- source: `number`
- coverage?: `table`
  - `nil` clears it.
  - respawnFee: `number`
    - Share of the bill covered, 0 to 1.
  - label?: `string`
    - Up to 32 bytes, shown in the emergency menu.

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `string`
  - `'invalid_player'`, `'invalid_coverage'`, `'not_loaded'`, `'owned_by_other_resource'`

```lua
exports.opx_medical:SetCoverage(source, { respawnFee = 1, label = 'Trauma Team Gold' })
```

## GetCoverage

```lua
exports.opx_medical:GetCoverage(source)
```

- source: `number`

**Returns**

- coverage?: `{ respawnFee: number, label?: string }`

## GetCalls

Every open call for help, oldest first.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:GetCalls()
```

**Returns**

- calls: `table[]`
  - id: `integer`
  - source: `number`
    - The downed player.
  - position: `{ x: number, y: number, z: number }`
  - bucket: `integer`
  - at: `integer`
  - responder?: `number`

## AckCall

A responder takes a patient's call; the patient is told a unit is on its way.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:AckCall(patientSource, responderSource)
```

- patientSource: `number`
- responderSource: `number`

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `'invalid_player'`, `'no_call'` or `'already_taken'`

## ReleaseCall

Gives a taken call back so another responder may take it.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:ReleaseCall(patientSource, responderSource)
```

- patientSource: `number`
- responderSource: `number`

**Returns**

- ok: `boolean`
- reason?: `'invalid_player'`, `'no_call'` or `'not_responder'`

## GetResponderCount

On-duty players of a `Config.responderJobType` job, downed ones left out.

```lua
exports.opx_medical:GetResponderCount()
```

**Returns**

- count: `integer`
