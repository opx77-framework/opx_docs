### opx:medical:down

A player went down.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:medical:down', function(source, info) end)
```

- source: `number`
- info: `table`
  - cause: `string`
  - killer?: `number`
  - position?: `{ x: number, y: number, z: number }`
  - bucket: `integer`
  - at: `integer`

### opx:medical:up

A downed player is up again, or his character left while down.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:medical:up', function(source, reason, by) end)
```

- source: `number`
- reason: `string`
  - `'revived'`, `'respawned'`, `'external'`, `'unloaded'`
- by?: `number`
  - The reviver, when given to `Revive`.

### opx:medical:call

A downed player called for help.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:medical:call', function(source, call) end)
```

- source: `number`
- call: `table`
  - Same shape as a `GetCalls` row.

### opx:medical:callAck

A responder took the call.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:medical:callAck', function(source, responderSource) end)
```

- source: `number`
- responderSource: `number`

### opx:medical:callClosed

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:medical:callClosed', function(source, callId, reason) end)
```

- source: `number`
- callId: `integer`
- reason: `string`
  - An `opx:medical:up` reason, or `'renewed'` (the player sent a new call).
