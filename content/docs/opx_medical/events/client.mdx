### opx:medical:down

The local player went down. Sent again after a client restart while down; later updates are internal.

```lua
RegisterNetEvent('opx:medical:down', function(state) end)
```

- state: `table`
  - respawnIn: `integer`
    - Seconds.
  - canRespawn: `boolean`
  - responders: `integer`
  - called: `boolean`
  - acked: `boolean`
  - fee: `integer`
    - Hospital bill now, coverage applied.
  - coverageLabel?: `string`
  - bleedoutIn?: `integer`
    - Seconds before the automatic respawn.

### opx:medical:up

```lua
RegisterNetEvent('opx:medical:up', function(reason) end)
```

- reason: `string`
  - `'revived'`, `'respawned'`, `'external'`, `'unloaded'`
